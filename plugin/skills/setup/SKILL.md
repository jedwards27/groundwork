# Set up Groundwork

1. Ask the person for their name, the email address they use for project mail, and, optionally, their company.
2. Call `setup` with those. They become the named person who approves asks and confirms drafts on this computer.
3. Call `projects` and show what's there. The Lake Mariner demo project is always loaded.
4. Give them the Groundwork app link from the result. Explain in one sentence that it's where they approve, confirm and check receipts, that it's only on this computer, and that everything runs in dry run.
5. Offer two next steps: the two-minute demo (`/groundwork:demo`), or starting one of their own projects (`/groundwork:new-project`).
